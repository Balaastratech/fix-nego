### Winners / Losers Analysis

**Winners**

1. **Enterprise procurement (clearest economics).** Pactum raised a $54M Series C (June 2025, Insight Partners; total >$100M) after 2.5x ARR growth and 25+ new Global 2000 customers ([pactum.com](https://pactum.com/blog/news-pactum-secures-54-million-in-series-c-funding-to-scale-agentic-ai-in-procurement)). Walmart's deployment delivered ~3% average gain, 68–72% of invited suppliers closing with the AI, 83% supplier satisfaction ([ai2roi case study](https://ai2roi.substack.com/p/ai-to-roi-case-study-walmarts-autonomous)). Largest autonomous deal ~$100M; fastest closed in 87 seconds ([estonianworld](https://estonianworld.com/business/estonian-ai-firm-pactum-secures-54m-in-funding-round-led-by-us-investor/)). Why it wins: savings are measurable, enabling gainshare pricing that removes buyer risk.

2. **B2B sales conversation intelligence (biggest spend pool).** Conversation-intelligence software market estimated ~$21.9–25.3B in 2025 depending on methodology ([futuremarketinsights](https://www.futuremarketinsights.com/reports/conversation-intelligence-software-market), [openpr/MRFR](https://www.openpr.com/news/4298706/conversation-intelligence-software-market-is-growing-at-a-cagr)). Gong extracts $1,200–$2,400/seat/yr plus mandatory platform fees of $5K–$50K and $7.5K–$65K implementation ([marketbetter](https://marketbetter.ai/blog/gong-pricing-breakdown-2026/), [sybill](https://www.sybill.ai/blogs/gong-pricing)). Note: Gong's March 2025 repricing unbundled forecasting/analytics into paid modules — pricing power signal ([getmaxiq](https://www.getmaxiq.com/blog/gong-ai-pricing)). Real-time coaching specifically (Balto/Cresta) sells at $40K–$150K/yr contracts into contact centers ([alpharun](https://www.alpharun.com/blog/balto-ai-pricing), [checkthat.ai](https://checkthat.ai/brands/cresta/pricing)).

3. **Job-seeker interview copilots (fast B2C revenue, ethically contested).** Cluely: verified $5.2M ARR in 2025 (founder publicly admitted the claimed $7M was a lie) ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cluely)). Final Round AI claims 10M+ users ([finalroundai.com](https://www.finalroundai.com/)) and raised a $6.88M oversubscribed seed (Feb 2025, Uncork) ([finalroundai blog](https://www.finalroundai.com/blog/final-round-ai-raises-6-88m-to-revolutionize-job-searching-with-ai)); pricing is aggressive ($81–$148/mo). The crowded copy-cat field (Verve, LockedIn, Sensei, Interview Sidekick) at $15–$120/mo proves demand elasticity.

4. **High-ticket human-led salary negotiation.** Levels.fyi sells flat packages at exactly $1,250 (Standard/Startup, $10k guaranteed increase), $2,450 (Premium, $15k), $5,000 (Leadership, $40k), money-back guaranteed ([levels.fyi/services](https://www.levels.fyi/services/)). Rora and Candor charge a contingent % of the negotiated increase, pay-nothing-if-no-increase ([teamrora.com](https://www.teamrora.com/), [candor.co](https://candor.co/negotiation)). This proves individuals pay 4 figures when stakes are 5-6 figures — but it's services revenue, not software.

**Losers / cautionary cases**

- **DoNotPay (consumer "AI negotiates your bills/legal")**: FTC final order Jan 2025, $193K monetary relief, prohibited from unsubstantiated "AI lawyer" claims, must notify 2021–2023 subscribers ([ftc.gov](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2025/02/ftc-finalizes-order-donotpay-prohibits-deceptive-ai-lawyer-claims-imposes-monetary-relief-requires)). Failure mode: overclaiming autonomous capability to consumers in regulated domains.
- **Cluely's trust problem**: 83,000 users' transcripts/screen captures breached mid-2025 (admin password in public GitHub repo); "undetectability" feature creates consent-law exposure under GDPR/two-party-consent states ([bluedothq](https://www.bluedothq.com/blog/cluely-review), [tldv](https://tldv.io/blog/cluely-review/)). Real-time stealth assistance monetizes but accumulates legal/platform risk.
- **Freelancer segment**: searches surfaced only blog demos and side projects, no funded company with published pricing — strongest evidence of a segment that talks but doesn't pay.
- **Recruiter-side negotiation**: buyers pay for compensation *data and benchmarking* (Compa, $52.1M raised, custom enterprise pricing, [cbinsights](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/compa)), not for negotiation conversation coaching. No proven standalone tool found.

### Pricing-Model Evidence

1. **Success-fee / gainshare wins wherever value is measurable**: Pactum (10–15% of savings, [getmonetizely](https://www.getmonetizely.com/blogs/how-ai-will-takeover-procurement-negotiations)), Rora/Candor (% of comp increase), Goodbill (15–20% of savings, $1k cap), Resolve (10–25% tiered). This is the dominant model in pure negotiation outcomes across B2B and B2C.
2. **Per-seat subscription wins for continuous-use B2B**: Gong/Balto/Cresta/Trellus, $60–$200/seat/mo typical, with platform fees as enterprise rent extraction.
3. **Flat per-event fee wins for episodic consumer purchases**: CarEdge $999/deal (with $2,200 avg savings as the value anchor) and the $149 one-time AI Negotiator ([caredge.com/concierge](https://caredge.com/concierge)); Levels.fyi flat tiers de-risked by guarantees.
4. **Monthly B2C subscriptions ($20–$148/mo) work only during active job search** — every interview-copilot vendor structures around short, intense usage windows (quarterly/semi-annual prepay discounts at Final Round are explicit churn management, [vervecopilot](https://www.vervecopilot.com/blog/verve-ai-final-round-ai-pricing-comparison)).

### Recommendation-Relevant Facts (for a small team)

- **B2C job-seeker is the only segment a small team has demonstrably cracked fast**: Cluely hit ~$5M ARR with a tiny team; Final Round scaled on a $6.88M seed. But it's churny, crowded, and reputationally hazardous (stealth-mode arms race, employer countermeasures).
- **Enterprise sales/contact-center coaching requires 50+ seat minimums, multi-week procurement, implementation services** ([checkthat.ai](https://checkthat.ai/brands/cresta/pricing)) — poor fit for a small team unless wedging in like Trellus did ($0→$59.99 self-serve atop existing dialers, [trellus.ai](https://www.trellus.ai/)). Trellus's freemium-on-top-of-Salesloft/Outreach/Apollo model is the proven small-team route into sales coaching.
- **Procurement gainshare is lucrative but enterprise-only** (Pactum's customers are Global 2000 with billions under management; sales cycles and integration depth are prohibitive for a small entrant).
- **Consumer negotiation pays only at high-stakes, infrequent events** (car ~$1K fee, medical bills % of savings, salary 4-figure/contingent). A small team can sell here with success-fee or flat-fee pricing, but unit economics depend on event acquisition cost; DoNotPay shows regulators punish capability overclaims.
- **Where budget already exists**: sales leaders (per-seat line items), procurement (savings budgets), individuals in career transitions. Where it does not: freelancers, and recruiters-as-negotiators.
