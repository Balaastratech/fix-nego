**Pattern:** clean bimodal split. Notetakers (Otter/Fireflies/Fathom/tl;dv/Sembly/Grain) are self-serve at $10–35/seat/mo. Revenue-intelligence and real-time platforms (Gong/Chorus/Balto/Cresta/Momentum/Attention) are sales-led at $1.2K–$1,800+/seat/yr with platform fees or $60K+ floors. There is almost nothing self-serve between $35 and $100/seat offering real-time guidance (Winn.ai at $59–69 is the closest).

## 2. Top Customer Complaints

- **Gong — post-call only, no live help:** "Post-call analysis limitations mean reps miss real-time coaching opportunities that could save deals during live conversations"; processing delay means no real-time guidance ([Oliv Gong reviews](https://www.oliv.ai/blog/gong-reviews), [Sybill Gong reviews](https://www.sybill.ai/blogs/gong-reviews)).
- **Gong — pricing opacity/hidden fees:** surprise platform fees, forced module bundling, 5–15% auto-renewal uplifts, no free trial, slow outsourced support; review titles include "Lots of Hype with Little To Show For It" ([MarketBetter Gong review](https://marketbetter.ai/blog/gong-review-2026/), [tl;dv Gong review roundup](https://tldv.io/blog/gong-review/)). Reports of contracts moving to a $50K platform-fee structure in early 2025 ([Sybill](https://www.sybill.ai/blogs/gong-pricing)).
- **Gong — usability/accuracy:** call-search difficulties were the #1 complaint (154 mentions in one 600-review analysis), then recording access, AI inaccuracy, accent problems ([Oliv](https://www.oliv.ai/blog/gong-reviews)).
- **Otter — accuracy + rogue bot:** #1 complaint is transcript accuracy with accents/crosstalk; speaker mislabeling ("Speaker 1/2"); widely reported OtterPilot joining meetings uninvited and auto-emailing transcripts to external participants ([eesel Otter reviews](https://www.eesel.ai/blog/otter-ai-reviews), [Bitdefender](https://www.bitdefender.com/en-us/blog/hotforsecurity/otter-ai-keeps-joining-your-meetings-uninvited-heres-how-to-make-it-stop)).
- **Fireflies — latency + speaker ID:** transcripts can take 10–15 min post-call; weak speaker identification; Teams-join failures ([tl;dv Fireflies review](https://tldv.io/blog/fireflies-review/), [usecarly comparison](https://www.usecarly.com/blog/otter-vs-fireflies-vs-fathom/)).
- **Fathom — glitches + review inflation:** recording glitches with crosstalk/accents; reviewers report being incentivized to leave positive G2 reviews ([alfred notetaker test](https://get-alfred.ai/blog/best-ai-meeting-notetakers), [tl;dv Fathom alternatives](https://tldv.io/blog/fathom-ai-notetaker-review-alternatives/)).
- **Chorus — bundle pressure:** ZoomInfo data bundles add $15K–40K/yr for unneeded features ([MarketBetter Chorus](https://marketbetter.ai/blog/chorus-ai-pricing-breakdown-2026/)).
- **Balto — prompt timing/UX:** real-time suggestions "can lag slightly or misinterpret filler words"; prompt cards obstruct agents' required screen content; sometimes misses what agents say ([G2 Balto reviews](https://www.g2.com/products/balto/reviews), via [Prospeo summary](https://prospeo.io/s/balto-pricing-reviews-pros-and-cons)).
- **Winn.ai — price for what it is:** $69/user/mo seen as expensive vs. notetakers; no free plan ([Claap Winn pricing](https://www.claap.io/blog/winn-pricing)).
- **Attention — maturity:** newer product, features still maturing; Salesforce-first with HubSpot secondary; weak call search ([G2 Attention](https://www.g2.com/products/attention/reviews), [SelectHub](https://www.selecthub.com/p/conversation-intelligence-software/attention-com/)).

## 3. Real-time vs Post-call Landscape

**Post-call only (analytics after the fact):** Gong, Chorus, Fireflies, Fathom, tl;dv, Sembly, Grain, Momentum. Gong's June 2026 "Mission Andromeda" adds AI coaching/chatbot but is still positioned around insights and workflow, not live in-call cue cards ([VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/gong-launches-mission-andromeda-with-ai-sales-coaching-chatbot-and-open-mcp)). tl;dv's "AI coaching/objection handling" on the $59 Business tier is post-call scoring against playbooks, not live ([tl;dv pricing](https://tldv.io/app/pricing/)). Otter offers real-time captions/transcription but not coaching ([index.dev comparison](https://www.index.dev/blog/otter-vs-fireflies-vs-fathom-ai-meeting-notes-comparison)).

**True real-time guidance:**
- **Balto** — live checklists, objection prompts, real-time QA for contact centers; ~$100–200/agent/mo, quote-only ([Balto real-time guidance](https://www.balto.ai/real-time-guidance/), [Alpharun](https://www.alpharun.com/blog/balto-ai-pricing)).
- **Cresta** — real-time agent assist + virtual agents, enterprise contact centers; ~$60K–150K/yr ([Cresta press](https://cresta.com/press/cresta-closes-125m-series-d-to-accelerate-adoption-of-human-centric-ai-in-the-contact-center)).
- **Winn.ai** — real-time playbook tracking + live CRM capture during meetings, $59–69/seat/mo — the cheapest real-time option found ([winn.ai](https://winn.ai/)).
- **Attention** — markets "real-time coaching" with MEDDIC/BANT scoring; G2 reviewers call real-time coaching "genuinely useful," but much of its value prop is post-call automation (60-second follow-up emails, CRM fill) ([attention.com](https://www.attention.com/), [RevOps Tools](https://revops.tools/attention-ai/)).
- Others entering the gap: Nomi.so, Clari Copilot (battlecards), Outreach Kaia, Salesken ([Nomi Gong-alternatives post](https://www.nomi.so/blog/2025-11-05-gong-alternatives-real-time-sales-coaching)).

**The pricing of real-time is 5–20x post-call:** live guidance today is either contact-center enterprise ($60K–150K/yr) or sales-led mid-market ($1.8K–4.8K/seat/yr at Attention's reported tiers), versus $120–420/seat/yr for post-call notetakers.

## 4. Negotiation-Specific Gap Analysis

- **No incumbent does live negotiation coaching for human sellers.** The closest categories: (a) **practice/roleplay sims** — Hyperbound, Second Nature-style tools, LavenirAI (procurement/sales negotiation avatars), Deelan ([LavenirAI](https://lavenirai.com/), [Hyperbound blog](https://www.hyperbound.ai/blog/top-10-ai-sales-coaching-platforms-in-2025)); (b) **prep/advisory chat** — Jenova's "AI negotiation coach" (claims 17K+ users) ([Jenova](https://www.jenova.ai/en/resources/ai-negotiation-coach-202605)); (c) **fully autonomous negotiation agents** — Pactum, which removes the human entirely and is procurement-only.
- **Pactum is the strongest demand signal for negotiation AI specifically:** $54M Series C (June 2025, Insight Partners), >$100M total raised, 2.5x ARR growth, 489% increase in spend negotiated, 25+ new Global-2000 customers ([Pactum press](https://pactum.com/blog/news-pactum-secures-54-million-in-series-c-funding-to-scale-agentic-ai-in-procurement)).
- **Demand signals for real-time coaching generally:** Cresta's $125M Series D at $1.6B valuation (Nov 2024) with ARR "nearly quadrupled" in two years ([Cresta](https://cresta.com/press/cresta-closes-125m-series-d-to-accelerate-adoption-of-human-centric-ai-in-the-contact-center), [PRNewswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/cresta-closes-125m-series-d-to-accelerate-adoption-of-human-centric-ai-in-the-contact-center-302309858.html)); MIT's 2025 AI Negotiation Summit findings that real-time AI feedback boosts negotiation performance and AI already beats humans at reading negotiation transcripts ([via Jenova](https://www.jenova.ai/en/resources/ai-negotiation-coach-202605)); Harvard PON covering AI's move "from agent to advisor" in negotiation ([PON](https://www.pon.harvard.edu/daily/negotiation-skills-daily/from-agent-to-advisor-how-ai-is-transforming-negotiation/)); negotiation training market ~$2B (2025), growing 7–8.5%/yr ([Jenova resources](https://www.jenova.ai/en/resources/best-ai-for-negotiation-coaching)).
- **The gap:** live, in-call negotiation/objection coaching for B2B sellers (pricing pushback, concession strategy, BATNA tracking) sold self-serve at notetaker-adjacent prices. Real-time incumbents (Balto/Cresta) are scripted-contact-center oriented with 50+ seat minimums; sales-meeting tools are post-call; negotiation AI is either roleplay-practice or fully autonomous procurement. Nobody combines (real-time) x (negotiation-specific) x (self-serve).
