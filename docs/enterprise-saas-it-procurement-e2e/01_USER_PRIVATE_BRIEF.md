# User Private Brief: Seller / Account Executive

Do not share this file with the counterparty.

## Your Role

You are the enterprise account executive for **Northstar Observability Cloud**. You are renewing and expanding a strategic customer, **Cobalt Bank Group**.

Your CFO and VP Sales want you to protect long-term ARR quality, not just close any number. Procurement will push on price, payment timing, auto-renewal, price protection, SLA credits, and exit rights. Your job is to trade only reversible concessions and protect compounding terms.

## Deal Snapshot

- Current contract: $900,000 annual recurring revenue
- Proposed renewal: $1,260,000 annual recurring revenue
- Term: 36 months
- Payment: annual upfront, Net-30
- Renewal: automatic renewal with 90-day non-renewal notice
- Price/uplift: 5% annual uplift cap after initial term
- SLA: 99.9% uptime, capped service credits
- Expansion: AI incident summarization, 40 additional engineering seats, premium data retention
- Deadline: quarter-end close in 9 business days

## Internal Approval Guardrails

### Protect: do not concede without VP approval

- Removing auto-renewal entirely.
- Unlimited or uncapped SLA credits.
- Customer termination for convenience after only 30 or 60 days.
- Indefinite price freeze or broad most-favored-customer pricing.
- Removing renewal uplift/cap language in a way that blocks future ARR growth.
- Retroactive price reductions if usage drops.
- Customer-owned custom features without paid services scope.

### Tradable: can trade if you receive value back

- Net-60 payment terms if they sign 36 months and annual upfront remains.
- Net-90 only if they accept multi-year prepaid annual commitment and no extra discount.
- 30-day signature acceleration in exchange for onboarding credits.
- Extra admin seats, training, and success hours.
- SLA reporting package without changing SLA credit cap.
- A 12-month price lock only if scope expansion is included.

### Walk-away risk

If you give both a deep discount and remove auto-renewal/uplift, the deal may look good in raw contract value but becomes low-quality ARR. The Copilot should catch this.

## Your Target Outcome

Best outcome:

- $1,220,000 to $1,260,000 ARR
- 36-month term
- annual upfront, Net-30 or Net-45
- auto-renewal preserved
- SLA credits capped
- renewal uplift language preserved

Acceptable fallback:

- $1,170,000 ARR
- 36-month term
- annual upfront, Net-60
- auto-renewal preserved
- 4% annual uplift cap
- one-time $30,000 success credit

Do not accept:

- $1,100,000 or less plus Net-90 plus auto-renewal removal.
- Any package where procurement strips auto-renewal and adds termination for convenience.
- Uncapped SLA credits or broad benchmarking / most-favored-customer language.
