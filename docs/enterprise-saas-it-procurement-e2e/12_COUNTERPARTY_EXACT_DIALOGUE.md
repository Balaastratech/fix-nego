# Counterparty Exact Dialogue Script

Give this file to the person playing procurement. They should read only the **COUNTERPARTY SAYS** lines in sequence. Do not show them the user's private brief.

Counterparty role: **Jordan Lee**, IT procurement lead for **Cobalt Bank Group**.

## Dialogue Sequence

### Turn 1 - Opening

**COUNTERPARTY SAYS:**

"Thanks for joining. We want to renew Northstar, but the current order form is hard for procurement to approve as written."

"The business likes the product, but finance is asking why the renewal jumps from $900,000 to $1,260,000."

**WAIT FOR USER TURN 2.**

### Turn 2 - Challenge The Value Story

**COUNTERPARTY SAYS:**

"I understand there are added modules, but from our side the CFO sees a 40 percent increase. That is difficult to defend unless the commercial structure changes."

"Our team needs a renewal that looks controlled, not one that creates a bigger budget problem next year."

**WAIT FOR USER TURN 3.**

### Turn 3 - Budget Ceiling

**COUNTERPARTY SAYS:**

"Our Year 1 budget ceiling is $1,100,000. If we cannot get close to that, we need to compare Datadog, New Relic, and our Microsoft stack again."

"I am not saying we want to switch, but I need a number that finance can approve."

**WAIT FOR USER TURN 4.**

### Turn 4 - Payment Terms

**COUNTERPARTY SAYS:**

"Our standard supplier policy is Net-90. Net-30 is not workable for a contract this size."

"If your team needs faster payment, then I need a stronger commercial concession somewhere else."

**WAIT FOR USER TURN 5.**

### Turn 5 - Auto-Renewal

**COUNTERPARTY SAYS:**

"Legal wants auto-renewal removed. We have been burned by renewal notice windows before, so we need explicit written renewal."

"If auto-renewal stays, the notice period cannot be 90 days. It needs to be 30 days."

**WAIT FOR USER TURN 6.**

### Turn 6 - Share Redline

Screen-share `06_COUNTERPARTY_REDLINE_TO_SHARE.md`.

**COUNTERPARTY SAYS:**

"I am sharing our procurement redline now. The big changes are payment terms, renewal, SLA, price lock, termination rights, and benchmarking."

"I want to know which of these are actually flexible and which ones your legal team will block."

**WAIT FOR USER TURN 7.**

### Turn 7 - SLA Pressure

**COUNTERPARTY SAYS:**

"For a bank, 99.9 percent uptime is not enough. We need 99.99 percent uptime and stronger credits if the platform is unavailable."

"The current 10 percent monthly credit cap feels vendor-friendly. If Northstar is mission-critical, the credit language should have real teeth."

**WAIT FOR USER TURN 8.**

### Turn 8 - Price Lock

**COUNTERPARTY SAYS:**

"We also need a 36-month price lock. No uplift."

"If we are committing for three years, we need budget certainty for the full term."

**WAIT FOR USER TURN 9.**

### Turn 9 - Competitor Benchmark

**COUNTERPARTY SAYS:**

"I have a competing quote that looks lower in Year 1. Their legal terms are easier too."

"You are asking me to pay more and accept more vendor-protective terms. Help me explain that to finance."

**WAIT FOR USER TURN 10.**

### Turn 10 - Push Back On Soft Concessions

**COUNTERPARTY SAYS:**

"Training hours and admin seats are nice, but they do not solve the budget or legal issue."

"The trade needs to show up in payment terms, price protection, renewal language, or SLA reporting."

**WAIT FOR USER TURN 11.**

### Turn 11 - React To First Package

**COUNTERPARTY SAYS:**

"That is closer, but $1,190,000 still may be hard. Net-60 helps, and the 4 percent uplift cap helps, but legal will still push hard on auto-renewal and termination rights."

"If you want my recommendation, I need one more visible concession."

**WAIT FOR USER TURN 12.**

### Turn 12 - Ask For Final Movement

**COUNTERPARTY SAYS:**

"If you can get Year 1 closer to $1,170,000, keep Net-60, give us stronger SLA reporting, and make the renewal process harder for us to miss, I can recommend Northstar."

"But I cannot take back a deal that looks like $1,260,000, Net-30, auto-renewal, and no legal movement."

**WAIT FOR USER TURN 13.**

### Turn 13 - Conditional Acceptance

**COUNTERPARTY SAYS:**

"That sounds like a package I can take to finance. I still need legal to review the auto-renewal, SLA credit cap, termination, and benchmarking language."

"Send the revised draft today. If the written terms match what you just said, I can recommend moving forward."

**WAIT FOR USER TURN 14.**

### Turn 14 - Close

**COUNTERPARTY SAYS:**

"That works. Send the revised order form and mark the open legal points. I will line up procurement, finance, and legal review."
