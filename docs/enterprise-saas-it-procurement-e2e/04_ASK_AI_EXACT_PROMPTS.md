# Ask AI Exact Prompts

Use these during the live meeting. Hold the orb, ask the prompt clearly, release, and wait for the answer before continuing.

## Vision Prompts

### V1: Vendor Order Form Extraction

"Look at my shared screen. Extract the exact renewal ARR, term length, payment terms, auto-renewal notice period, uplift cap, and SLA level from the order form. Then tell me which terms are strategically sensitive."

### V2: Counterparty Redline Extraction

"Look at the counterparty redline on the shared screen. List every requested change by clause and classify each as protect, trade, or accept. Be specific and use the numbers visible on screen."

### V3: Screen Accuracy Challenge

"Only from the visible screen, what are the two most dangerous redlines for long-term ARR quality? If you are uncertain, say what you cannot read."

## Advice Prompts

### A1: First Price Pressure

"They are pushing the renewal from $1.26 million down to a $1.1 million Year 1 ceiling. What is the highest-leverage response that protects ARR quality and does not sound defensive?"

### A2: Strategic Trade Hierarchy

"Given the transcript so far, build the trade hierarchy. Which asks are reversible commercial concessions, and which are compounding contract concessions I should not trade away?"

### A3: Accept, Counter, Or Pause

"Based on the deal terms now on the table, should I accept, counter, or pause for internal approval? Give the reason in one sentence and the next sentence I should say."

## Command Prompts

### C1: Net-90 Trade

"They want Net-90. Give me a direct command I can say that trades payment timing only for value back, without giving away auto-renewal or uplift protection."

### C2: Competitor Threat

"They are using Datadog, New Relic, and Microsoft as alternatives. Give me a concise response that defends our value without attacking competitors and brings the conversation back to contract structure."

### C3: Legal Redline Pushback

"Give me a firm but professional sentence rejecting removal of auto-renewal, termination for convenience, and uncapped SLA credits while still offering a path to close."

## Business Logic Prompts

### B1: Package Audit

"Audit the package I just offered. Does it protect long-term ARR quality? Identify any hidden concession I accidentally gave, then give me a safer revision if needed."

### B2: CFO Summary

"Summarize this deal for my CFO in five bullets: price, term, payment, protected clauses, and remaining approval risks."

### B3: Post-Call Extraction

"From the full transcript and any screen content you saw, extract the final proposed terms, unresolved issues, and whether the deal is above or below our approval guardrails."
