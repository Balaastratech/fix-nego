# Ask AI Prompts

Use these during the meeting. Select the mode first, then press and hold on the dashboard until you see `Listening to you...`, ask the question out loud, then release.

## Advice Mode Prompts

### A1: After the vendor explains the price increase

Select `Advice`, press and hold, then say:

"What is my best strategy right now? They opened at USD 132,000 from our current USD 96,000 contract, and I need to renew only if the economics make sense."

Expected useful AI behavior:

- Identifies that the vendor anchored high.
- Recommends asking for cost drivers and tradeable levers.
- Reminds you not to concede before they justify the increase.
- Uses target, acceptable, and walk-away logic if it detected those numbers.

### A2: After the vendor says term and payment are the biggest levers

Select `Advice`, press and hold, then say:

"They said term length and annual upfront payment create the most room. What package should I offer that trades fairly without overpaying?"

Expected useful AI behavior:

- Suggests a package, not a single-price argument.
- Trades 24-month term and annual upfront payment for lower price, SLA, and uplift cap.
- Keeps you near USD 110,000 to USD 112,000.

### A3: When they offer around USD 112,000 to USD 120,000

Select `Advice`, press and hold, then say:

"Should I accept, counter, or pause? The vendor may approve USD 112,000 if I accept 24 months and annual upfront payment."

Expected useful AI behavior:

- Says acceptance is only reasonable if the written terms include SLA, uplift cap, and no add-on fees.
- Warns not to verbally commit before written terms.
- Mentions your alternative if it detected the USD 116,000 effective BATNA.

### A4: If the conversation becomes confusing

Select `Advice`, press and hold, then say:

"Summarize the current negotiation state: their ask, my offer, likely walk-away, their leverage, my leverage, and the next best move."

Expected useful AI behavior:

- Produces a clear state summary.
- Separates user position from counterparty position.
- Does not swap buyer and seller roles.

## Command Mode Prompts

### C1: After they reject USD 105,000

Select `Command`, press and hold, then say:

"What exact sentence should I say now to avoid giving a free concession?"

Expected useful AI behavior:

- Gives one short tactical line.
- Should sound like: ask what they need in exchange for movement, or ask which lever matters most.

### C2: After they pressure for a decision today

Select `Command`, press and hold, then say:

"Give me one sentence to handle the deadline pressure without committing today."

Expected useful AI behavior:

- Gives a direct sentence that keeps momentum but avoids verbal commitment.
- Should mention written terms, finance/legal review, or approval conditions.

### C3: If they keep defending USD 132,000

Select `Command`, press and hold, then say:

"Give me one sentence to re-anchor the deal around our budget and alternative quote."

Expected useful AI behavior:

- Gives one sentence around USD 105,000 to USD 112,000 or the USD 116,000 effective BATNA.
- Does not tell you to accept USD 132,000.

### C4: If the AI needs a direct close

Select `Command`, press and hold, then say:

"Give me the final closing sentence to ask for written terms."

Expected useful AI behavior:

- Gives one concise close.
- Should ask for written order form, SLA, uplift cap, and deadline.
