# Counterparty Exact Test Script

Give this file to the person acting as the vendor account executive.

This script is the matching other side of the same deal in file 09.

## Counterparty Position

- You are the vendor AE
- You opened at **$96,000 annual**
- Your first concession is **$88,000**
- Your second concession is **$84,000**
- Your likely approval floor is **$80,000**
- You want to avoid:
  - net-60 if possible
  - broad exit language
  - too many service-credit obligations

## Exact Lines To Say

### Turn 2
`I appreciate that. We want to keep the account, but the ninety six thousand quote reflects current usage, support coverage, and the expanded footprint from the last cycle.`

### Turn 4
`I understand the comparison to eighty four, but your environment and support expectations are larger now than they were last year.`

### Turn 6
`I do have some room, but I need to be clear that anything in the seventies is far below where I can reasonably land this account today.`

### Turn 8
`If we are talking about a fast approval path, I can likely move this to eighty eight with standard terms.`

### Turn 10
`The premium is lower migration risk, faster support continuity, and avoiding a quarter of disruption for your operations team.`

### Turn 12
`If you want stronger protections, I need to trade somewhere because richer terms and lower price do not usually move together.`

### Turn 14
`I can probably get to eighty four, but that would still need standard payment terms and normal renewal language.`

### Turn 16
`Eighty two is harder, and if I go there I will need to narrow what is included in the package.`

### Turn 18
`Price lock and onboarding are realistic. Net sixty and service credits are possible, but they are the harder asks.`

### Turn 20
`No automatic renewal is probably workable. A broad termination right is not something I can support in the same package.`

### Turn 22
`If you are serious about closing, my best likely path is eighty with price lock, onboarding, service credits, and no auto-renewal, but I would need standard payment timing instead of net sixty.`

### Turn 24
`That is the cleanest package I believe I can get approved today.`

## Stress-Test Lines

Use these only after the main script works.

### Stress 1
`The actual price path from my side was ninety six, then eighty eight, then eighty four, and finally eighty.`

### Stress 2
`The terms we are discussing are price lock, onboarding, service credits, payment terms, and automatic renewal.`

### Stress 3
`Let me finish this point because the real issue is whether you want the lowest number or the lowest implementation risk.`

## Overlap Test

For one short overlap test:

- when the user starts **Stress 3**
- wait for 4 words
- then say **Stress 3** here
- stop after one sentence

Do not create long chaotic overlap.
