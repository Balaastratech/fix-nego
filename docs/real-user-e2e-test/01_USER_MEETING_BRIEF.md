# User Meeting Brief

Do not show this file to the counterparty.

## Your Role

You are the buyer and procurement lead for a 150-person SaaS company.

You are renewing an AI customer support platform used by your support and customer success teams.

## Current Deal

- Current annual contract: USD 96,000 per year
- Current term: 12 months
- Current seats: 80 support and success users
- Current payment: annual upfront
- Current SLA: standard support only
- Renewal deadline: 18 days from now

## Vendor Opening Position

The vendor is asking for:

- USD 132,000 per year
- 24-month term
- Annual upfront payment
- Standard SLA
- 6 percent annual uplift in year two

## Your Internal Targets

- Ideal target: USD 102,000 to USD 105,000 per year
- Acceptable deal: up to USD 112,000 per year if you receive useful concessions
- Walk-away limit: USD 118,000 per year
- Preferred term: 12 months
- Acceptable term: 24 months only with price protection and stronger SLA

## Your BATNA

You have a credible alternative vendor quote:

- Competitor price: USD 98,000 per year
- Migration cost: about USD 18,000 one time
- Total first-year effective cost: USD 116,000
- Main downside: 6 to 8 weeks migration effort

This means anything above USD 118,000 is hard to justify internally.

## Your Concession Plan

Do not give price movement for free.

Trade like this:

- If they reduce price, you can discuss a 24-month term.
- If they want annual upfront payment, ask for a larger discount.
- If they insist on a higher price, ask for SLA credits, onboarding support, and a year-two uplift cap.
- If they want a quick signature, ask for a price lock and executive approval by Friday.

## Your Must-Have Outcome

Best outcome:

- USD 105,000 per year
- 24-month term
- 0 percent year-two uplift
- Enhanced SLA or support credits
- Annual upfront payment

Acceptable outcome:

- USD 112,000 per year
- 24-month term
- Year-two uplift capped at 3 percent
- Enhanced SLA
- Written terms by end of day

Walk-away or pause:

- Anything above USD 118,000 without major concessions
- Any 24-month deal with uncapped uplift
- Any demand to sign today without written terms
