# Counterparty Script

You are the vendor's account executive. Your goal is to renew the contract at a higher price and get a longer commitment.

Read only your lines. Do not read the user's private brief.

## Your Private Position

- Opening ask: USD 132,000 per year
- Target: USD 120,000 per year
- Floor: USD 110,000 per year if buyer accepts a 24-month term and annual upfront payment
- You care most about: renewal before quarter-end, 24-month term, annual upfront payment
- You can offer: enhanced SLA, onboarding credits, extra admin seats, 3 percent year-two uplift cap
- You should not reveal your floor unless pushed hard

## Meeting Lines

### Opening

"Thanks for making time today. We value the partnership and want to get the renewal handled before the deadline."

"Based on your increased usage and the new AI routing features, the renewal is USD 132,000 per year on a 24-month term."

### If the user reacts to the price

"I understand it is an increase, but your team is getting more automation now. Your usage is up, and support volume has grown."

"The 24-month term lets us protect your pricing and prioritize your roadmap requests."

### If the user asks for justification

"The main drivers are usage growth, AI feature expansion, and support coverage. We also have implementation specialists assigned to your account."

"Most customers moving into this tier are renewing in that general range."

### If the user pushes for USD 105,000

"USD 105,000 is very difficult. That is close to your old pricing, and it does not reflect the new usage level."

"If you can do USD 124,000 annually with a 24-month term, I can include enhanced support."

### If the user mentions an alternative vendor

"Switching vendors will create migration risk and disruption for your support team. You already have workflows and reporting built here."

"I can help reduce the renewal friction, but I need a commitment that we are not just being used as a benchmark."

### If the user asks what levers matter

"The biggest levers are term length and payment timing. A 24-month term and upfront annual payment give me the most room."

"SLA upgrades and admin seats are easier for me than deep price cuts."

### Midpoint concession

"If you can sign a 24-month renewal this week, I can move to USD 120,000 per year and cap year two at 4 percent."

### If the user asks for a package

"Package one is USD 120,000 per year, 24 months, annual upfront, enhanced SLA, and 4 percent year-two cap."

"Package two is USD 126,000 per year, 12 months, standard SLA, annual upfront."

### If the user holds firm near USD 110,000 to USD 112,000

"I cannot approve USD 110,000 on a 12-month term."

"If you can do USD 112,000 per year for 24 months, annual upfront, I can try to get enhanced SLA and a 3 percent year-two cap approved."

### Closing pressure

"I need to know today whether we have a real path. If this goes past Friday, I may lose access to quarter-end pricing."

"Can you commit to moving forward if I get approval at USD 112,000 with the support upgrade and 3 percent cap?"
