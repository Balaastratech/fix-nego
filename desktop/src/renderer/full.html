Welcome — here's how it works

🟢

Wait for the green light

When you open the app it connects to the AI services. The dot on the floating orb (and the banner here) shows the status: red = connecting, amber = getting ready, green = ready. The Start Session button stays locked until everything is ready.

🪟

Pick your meeting window

On the Dashboard, choose the window of your meeting app (Zoom, Teams, Meet). Your mic, audio routing, and reply output are set up automatically. Then click Start Session.

✦

The floating orb

Click & drag (left or right button) to move it anywhere. Left-click and hold the orb (or your global hold key) to privately ask the AI — your mic is muted to the meeting while you hold. Double right-click the orb to open this main window. A single right-click does nothing, so you won't open it by accident.

⌨️

Global shortcut (hold-to-ask)

You can ask the AI without clicking the orb by holding the global hold key from anywhere — even while the meeting app is focused. Release to send your question. For meeting mute to work, enable your meeting app's global mute shortcut (e.g. Zoom → Settings → Keyboard Shortcuts → "Enable Global Shortcut" for Mute).

🔑

Where to save your API keys

Open the Settings tab (top of this window). Paste your provider keys there and click Save settings. Keys are stored locally on this machine and never shown again. Use Test to verify a key before saving.

🌐

Choosing your language

Use the Language card on the Dashboard (or the language chip on the orb). Set what to transcribe, what language the AI replies in, and how it's shown on screen. "Auto" covers 10 languages; pin a language for others.
